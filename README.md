# Cool-Monkey-Swings
A Game for HackClub Haven

## Our game:

Our game is a simple monkey maze runner, and it is a time trial!!! 
Every time you drop into the win pit, you get +1 score to your round counter, but every time you die, you loose a round...

We have custom made soundtracks in GarageBand, and a custom map for your monkey. You will find surprises in the map, like holes down to the 'void' and parts where you have to climb into trees!

## How to play:

We will time you with one minute on the clock, to score as high as possible and get an honourable title of Professional Monkey larper.

However, here are some REGULATIONS to play:

## Regulations:

You must play holding a banana in you hands, kind of using it as a joystick to control the arrow keys. Be one with the real monkeys and have fun!
